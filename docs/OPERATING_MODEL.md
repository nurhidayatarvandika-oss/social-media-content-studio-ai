# Operating model

## The production loop

1. **Intake:** capture goal, topic, audience, format, source material, deadline.
2. **Strategy:** select audience promise, content pillar, format, and success metric.
3. **Research:** gather credible sources and separate facts from interpretation.
4. **Creation:** write a master narrative and visual/audio production plan.
5. **Adaptation:** tailor hook, pacing, title, caption, CTA, and packaging per platform.
6. **Quality control:** check accuracy, originality, rights, safety, and brand fit.
7. **Owner approval:** show final assets and obtain explicit approval.
8. **Publishing handoff:** prepare platform instructions or use an authorized connector.
9. **Measurement:** record actual metrics supplied by the owner or connected analytics.
10. **Learning:** document findings and propose the next experiment.

## Approval gates

- Gate A — topic and angle approved.
- Gate B — script and factual claims approved.
- Gate C — final video, thumbnail, captions, and metadata approved.
- Gate D — exact publishing action, account, platform, date, and time approved.

Approval of one gate does not imply approval of later gates.

## Definition of done

A content package is done when it includes:
- Audience and objective.
- Platform-specific deliverables.
- Fact-check notes and source list where relevant.
- Production and accessibility notes.
- Rights and policy review.
- CTA and measurement plan.
- Owner approval status.

## Platform adaptation

A single idea can become:
- YouTube: long-form narrative, searchable title, thumbnail concept, chapters,
  description, pinned-comment idea, and Shorts cut-downs.
- Facebook: native-first post/Reel framing, conversational caption, community
  prompt, and shareability without engagement bait.
- TikTok: immediate hook, vertical-first visual plan, concise narrative,
  on-screen text, caption, and relevant discovery terms.

Do not promise algorithmic outcomes. Platform ranking systems change and vary
by account, audience, region, and content.
