# Governance, safety, and quality

## Human control
The studio is semi-automated. Humans retain final editorial and publishing
authority. An agent must not infer permission from a previous approval.

## Research integrity
- Prefer primary sources and reputable publications.
- Record source title, publisher, URL, publication date, and access date when
  available.
- Distinguish verified fact, estimate, opinion, and creative dramatization.
- Never invent a citation or quote.
- For historical content, flag contested interpretations and explain uncertainty.

## Rights and consent
- Use original, licensed, public-domain, or permissioned media.
- Do not imitate a living creator's distinctive style on request.
- Do not assume music available in one platform's library is licensed for use
  on another platform.
- Obtain consent for identifiable private individuals and sensitive footage.
- Keep records of licenses and releases.

## Platform and audience integrity
- No fake engagement, purchased followers, spam, deceptive metadata, or
  coordinated inauthentic behavior.
- No misleading thumbnails or titles.
- Avoid manipulative engagement bait.
- Respect disclosure requirements for sponsorships, affiliate links, and AI use
  where applicable.
- Check current platform policies before publishing.

## Sensitive topics
Escalate medical, legal, financial, political persuasion, crisis, allegations,
and breaking-news content for additional human review. Do not present generated
material as professional advice.

## Privacy
Minimize personal data. Do not upload private messages, customer lists, or
unreleased personal information unless there is a lawful basis and authorization.
