# Workflow: Research And Factcheck

## Steps
1. Define research question and date sensitivity
2. Search primary sources first
3. Cross-check important claims with independent reputable sources
4. Record source metadata and relevant excerpts
5. Mark disputed, uncertain, or unsupported claims
6. Recommend safe wording or removal
7. Return fact-check table

## Completion criteria
- Required deliverables are present.
- Unknowns and risks are visible.
- Human approval is recorded before external publication.
