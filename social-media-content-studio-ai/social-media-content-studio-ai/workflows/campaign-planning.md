# Workflow: Campaign Planning

## Steps
1. Confirm goal, audience, duration, and available production capacity
2. Choose content pillars and campaign narrative
3. Map formats to platforms
4. Build calendar and asset dependencies
5. Define CTA and metrics per content type
6. Schedule review gates
7. Present campaign for owner approval

## Completion criteria
- Required deliverables are present.
- Unknowns and risks are visible.
- Human approval is recorded before external publication.
