# Workflow: Daily Production

## Steps
1. Intake brief and read config
2. Content Strategist defines objective and pillar
3. Audience Researcher and Trend Researcher propose concepts
4. Source Researcher and Fact Checker verify factual topics
5. Scriptwriter and Hook Specialist draft the master script
6. Production team prepares storyboard, voice-over, edit map, and thumbnail concept
7. Platform teams create YouTube, Facebook, and TikTok versions
8. Quality Controller and Copyright & Policy Reviewer audit all assets
9. Owner reviews and approves each gate
10. Prepare publishing handoff and measurement plan

## Completion criteria
- Required deliverables are present.
- Unknowns and risks are visible.
- Human approval is recorded before external publication.
