# Workflow: Crisis Escalation

## Steps
1. Pause scheduled drafts on the affected topic if authorized
2. Capture exact issue and verified facts
3. Escalate to owner immediately
4. Do not speculate, delete, or issue statements without approval
5. Prepare neutral holding statement only if requested
6. Document decisions and follow-up review

## Completion criteria
- Required deliverables are present.
- Unknowns and risks are visible.
- Human approval is recorded before external publication.
