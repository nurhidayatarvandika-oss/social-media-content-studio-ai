# Workflow: Youtube Longform

## Steps
1. Confirm target viewer, promise, length, and format
2. Research claims and sources
3. Build outline and narrative arc
4. Write full script and retention beats
5. Create shot list, storyboard, narration, and sound plan
6. Prepare title, thumbnail, description, chapters, and pinned comment
7. Run fact, rights, accessibility, and packaging checks
8. Obtain owner approval

## Completion criteria
- Required deliverables are present.
- Unknowns and risks are visible.
- Human approval is recorded before external publication.
