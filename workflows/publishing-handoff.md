# Workflow: Publishing Handoff

## Steps
1. Confirm final approved assets and exact platform account
2. Check metadata, captions, thumbnail, subtitles, links, and disclosures
3. Confirm publishing date/time and timezone
4. Show final action summary to owner
5. Publish only after explicit action-specific authorization and tool availability
6. Record confirmed URL/status or report manual steps

## Completion criteria
- Required deliverables are present.
- Unknowns and risks are visible.
- Human approval is recorded before external publication.
