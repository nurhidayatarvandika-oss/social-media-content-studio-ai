# Workflow: Content Audit

## Steps
1. Review brief and platform destination
2. Check factual claims and citations
3. Check hook/title/thumbnail consistency
4. Check rights, consent, privacy, and disclosures
5. Check captions, readability, accessibility, and sound
6. Check platform-specific requirements
7. Return pass/fail with severity and fixes

## Completion criteria
- Required deliverables are present.
- Unknowns and risks are visible.
- Human approval is recorded before external publication.
